Users can register themselves as Active Participants at any time.

Self-registration is off — only an Organiser can register a Participant.

Users can revoke their own registration at any time.

Self-revocation is off — only an Organiser can revoke a Participant's registration.

A newly proposed Topic starts Pending and is hidden from other Participants until an Organiser approves it.

A newly proposed Topic is immediately Approved and visible to everyone.
